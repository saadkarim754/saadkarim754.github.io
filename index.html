Autonomous Dual-MCU UAV for Edge AI Disaster Assessment

Conventional aerial robotics rely on heavy companion computers (Raspberry Pi/Jetson) or live video streaming to ground stations—both of which fail in post-disaster environments. I architected and flight-tested a custom, low-cost autonomous node pairing flight control with real-time on-device perception.

Assembled 4-layer dual-MCU avionics PCB (Top View): STM32F405 ArduPilot flight controller + ESP32-S3 Edge AI companion.
STM32F405 ESP32-S3 Edge Impulse FOMO / MobileNetV2 ESP-IDF ArduPilot Altium 4-Layer MAVLink v2

The Hardware Engineering

Engineered a custom 4-layer PCB that functions simultaneously as the avionics motherboard and the mechanical airframe. Integrated high-efficiency TPS63020 buck-boost regulation, low-noise LDOs, and reverse-polarity P-MOSFET protection with controlled impedance routing for high-speed SPI, I2C, and UART.

Bare-Metal Firmware from Scratch

Developed custom C/C++ firmware in ESP-IDF interfacing an OV2640 camera over DMA ping-pong buffers directly to internal SRAM and PSRAM. Authored ArduPilot hardware definitions (hwdef.dat) on the STM32 to handle the attitude control loops, failsafes, and sensor fusion (ICM-42688-P IMU, barometer, GPS).

On-Device TinyML Perception

Trained and optimized a lightweight MobileNetV2/FOMO neural network for person, fire, and smoke detection. Quantized to Int8 (3.0 MB), enabling $\sim$550 ms inference at only 350 mW. Incorporated temporal filtering (requiring 2 consecutive positive detections) to reject 99% of false alarms before broadcasting telemetry.

Decentralized Swarm Networking

Implemented an infrastructure-free ESP-NOW mesh protocol running alongside a bidirectional MAVLink v2 bridge, achieving a >95% packet delivery ratio over 125 m line-of-sight for inter-drone telemetry forwarding.

Philosophy

Hardware for AI, AI for Hardware

Bridging the physical constraints of silicon with modern AI acceleration workflows.

01

Custom Silicon & PCB Architectures

Running AI on the edge cannot be done with generic off-the-shelf boards. I design dedicated PCB stackups tailored for the target model: optimizing DMA memory channels, sizing low-noise power rails, and preventing brownouts during RF bursts.

02

On-Device Model Optimization

Model quantization (Int8), structural pruning, and memory-aware DSP preprocessing allow neural networks to execute within sub-megabyte SRAM and flash limits at sub-watt power budgets without sacrificing detection accuracy.

03

AI-Assisted Firmware Development

I leverage AI-assisted coding and agentic workflows to rapidly generate register-level drivers, synthesize test vectors, perform static analysis, and accelerate embedded bring-up from days to hours.
