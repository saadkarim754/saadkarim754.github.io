About

As an Electrical Engineering student at PIEAS, my drive for innovation is fueled by a deep fascination with aeronautics, mirroring the inventive spirit of my Marvel heroes.

Currently, I’m turning that enthusiasm into reality at Saf Shikan, where I engineering custom ESP32-based flight controllers and researching autonomous UAV swarms for disaster assessment. My hands-on journey ranges from rigorous avionics testing at PAC Kamra to prototyping educational STEM kits at NASTP.

I enjoy bridging the gap between hardware and software—whether it's designing PCBs for 65g mini-drones, calibrating Pixhawk systems, or coding control algorithms in Python and C++. I am passionate about building practical systems that fly, solve problems, and push the boundaries of embedded engineering.
